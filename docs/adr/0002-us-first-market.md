# ADR 0002 - US-first market with a New Zealand chef

Date: 2026-11-04. Status: Accepted (owner confirmed).

## Context
The chef is based in New Zealand. Search demand data (OpenSEO / DataForSEO, 2026-11-04) shows the US is far larger than New Zealand for every target term checked.

## Evidence (monthly searches, US vs NZ)

| Query | US | NZ |
|---|---|---|
| lazy dinner ideas | 18,100 | 170 |
| what to eat when sick | 14,800 | 210 |
| leftover chicken recipes | 14,800 | 720 |
| leftover ham recipes | 27,100 | 390 |
| fiber calculator | 2,900 | 20 |
| recipe cost calculator | 880 | 20 |
| food cost calculator | 1,900 | 50 |

NZ-specific terms exist at small scale (for example "kumara recipes" 880, "leftover lamb recipes" 260, "cheap dinner ideas nz" 260).

## Decision
Target a US-first English-speaking audience. The NZ-based chef is the author and differentiator, not the target market.

## Consequences
- Show metric and US customary units side by side.
- Use US-relevant food-safety guidance for leftovers (final authority TBD: USDA vs NZ MPI).
- Seasonal content follows the US calendar; NZ seasons are reversed (photography and timing need planning).
- A New Zealand dietitian's credentials may carry less weight with US readers (unverified).

## Unknowns
- Currency for cost tools (USD vs NZD).
- Whether a secondary NZ section is worth adding later.
