## Scope (build exactly this, nothing more)

### 1. Grocery data module (tests first)
**`website/src/data/usda-food-plans.ts`** (create the `data` folder): a typed, immutable dataset transcribed EXACTLY from `gan-harness/sources/grocery.md` section 2 (September 2026). Include: `asOf` ("2026-09", shown as "September 2026"), the source report names and URLs, retrieval date, and for each of the four plans a list of 15 groups `{ id, label, sex: 'any'|'female'|'male', minAge, maxAge, weekly, monthly }` with BOTH printed columns stored as printed (numbers in dollars, e.g. 26.3 and 114.2).
- Age bands. Children (sex 'any'): 1, 2-3, 4-5, 6-8, 9-11. Female and male each: 12-13, 51-70, 71+ (maxAge 120) for all plans; **Thrifty uses 14-19 and 20-50; the other three plans use 14-18 and 19-50.** Age 19 therefore maps to the teen band in Thrifty and the adult band in the other plans. This is correct: do not "fix" it.
- Do NOT derive monthly from weekly. USDA prints monthly from unrounded weekly values (26.30 x 4.333 = 113.96 but USDA prints 114.20). Sum the printed weekly column and the printed monthly column separately.

**`website/tests/usda-food-plans.test.ts`** must check: 15 groups per plan; every whole age 1 to 120 maps to exactly one group per plan for each sex (and ages under 1 map to none); the boundary ages 1, 2, 4, 6, 9, 11, 12, 13, 14, 18, 19, 20, 50, 51, 70, 71, 120 land in the right band for each plan; the Thrifty reference family (male 20-50, female 20-50, child 6-8, child 9-11) weekly sums to exactly 236.30 and monthly sums to within 0.10 of 1,024.00 (printed 1,024.00; the printed monthly column sums to 1,023.90 because of USDA's rounding); at least 8 spot-checked values copied from different rows and plans in the source table; all values are positive and finite; a monthly value is within 1.5 percent of weekly x 4.333 for every row (a transcription sanity check, not a derivation).

### 2. Grocery calculator logic (tests first)
**`website/src/lib/calculators/grocery-budget.ts`**
- Input: a list of household members `{ age, sex }` (strings as typed or numbers). `sex` is needed only for age 12 and over; for ages 1-11 it is ignored.
- Validation (per member, result objects, never throw, one bad member does not hide the others): age must be a whole number from 1 to 120 (ages under 1 get a specific message that infants are not covered by the USDA plans); sex required for age 12+; reject blank, non-numeric, NaN/Infinity, decimals, negatives. A fully blank member row is ignored, not an error. At most 12 members; at least one valid member is needed for a result.
- Calculation per plan: sum the members' printed weekly costs and, separately, their printed monthly costs, then multiply each sum by the USDA household-size factor, where household size = the number of VALID members: 1 -> 1.20, 2 -> 1.10, 3 -> 1.05, 4 -> 1.00, 5 or 6 -> 0.95, 7 or more -> 0.90 (quoted rule in the source notes; the factor applies to the total after summing). If some members are invalid, still compute from the valid ones but report the errors and make clear in the result that the total excludes them.
- Output per plan: `{ plan, weekly, monthly }` rounded to the cent only at the end (reuse `formatUsd` from `recipe-cost.ts`; do not duplicate it), plus the factor used and the as-of month.
- Tests must cover every boundary age, every household-size factor (1 to 7+, including 4 = no adjustment), one hand-computed household per plan (show the arithmetic in a test comment, e.g. 2 adults + 2 children, Thrifty weekly), mixed valid and invalid members, blank members, 12 and 13 members, infants, ages 0, 121, 1.5, "abc", "", sex missing for an adult, sex given for a child (ignored), and a single member.

### 3. Fiber logic (tests first)
**`website/src/lib/calculators/fiber.ts`**
- IOM Adequate Intake (AI) for total fiber in grams per day, from `gan-harness/sources/fiber.md` section 1 (National Academies DRI summary table, 2002/2005): ages 4-8 -> 25 (both sexes); 9-13 -> male 31, female 26; 14-18 -> male 38, female 26; 19-30 -> male 38, female 25; 31-50 -> male 38, female 25; 51-70 -> male 30, female 21; over 70 (71+) -> male 30, female 21. Ages 1-3 (19 g) are NOT offered; age under 4 returns a clear "this calculator covers ages 4 and over" message. Pregnancy and lactation are not offered.
- Input: `age`, `sex` ('female'|'male'; required), optional `calories`.
- Calorie-based figure: `14 x calories / 1000`, rounded to the nearest whole gram, ONLY for age 19 and over and only when calories is given. The site sets the accepted calorie range, 1,000 to 4,000 kcal, and says on the page that this range is the site's own limit and not from the source. Outside the range, or for age under 19, return a clear message instead of a number. Blank calories means no calorie figure and no error.
- Output: `{ aiGrams, band label, calorieGrams?, calorieNote? }`. Result objects, never throw. Validate age (whole number, 4 to 120), sex, calories (finite number); reject NaN/Infinity/negative/decimal ages.
- Tests must cover every band and sex, the boundary ages 4, 8, 9, 13, 14, 18, 19, 30, 31, 50, 51, 70, 71, 120, age 3 and 121 and decimals and text, calorie edge cases (999, 1000, 4000, 4001, blank, "abc", "2,000" with a comma which should give the same helpful comma message style as the recipe calculator, whitespace), age under 19 with calories given, rounding (for example 2,500 kcal -> 35 g, 1,800 kcal -> 25 g, 2,250 kcal -> 31.5 -> 32 with round half up), and that the AI number never depends on calories.

### 7. Source notes and limits (visible text on each page)
