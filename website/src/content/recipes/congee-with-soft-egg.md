steps:
  - "Put the rinsed rice, water, ginger and salt in a pot and bring to a boil, then turn the heat down to a low simmer."
  - "Simmer uncovered for 30 to 35 minutes, stirring every few minutes so nothing sticks, until the rice has broken down into a thick, creamy porridge. Add a splash of hot water if it gets too thick."
  - "Make two small wells in the congee and crack an egg into each. Turn off the heat, cover, and leave for 3 to 4 minutes until the whites are just set and the yolks are still soft."
  - "Ladle into two bowls and finish with the spring onion, soy sauce and sesame oil."

Congee is the dish I make when I want something warm and gentle with almost no effort. The long, slow simmer does the work, and the egg poaches itself in the residual heat. Make it plain, then dress it up with whatever you have in the fridge.
