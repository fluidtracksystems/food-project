  - "Rice way: turn the heat to high, add the cold rice and peas, and fry for 4 to 5 minutes, pressing the rice against the pan. Stir through the soy sauce and serve."
  - "Pasta way: cook the pasta in salted water until just tender, scoop out 120 ml (1/2 cup) of the cooking water, then drain. Add the pasta, peas, parmesan and a splash of the water to the base and toss over low heat for 2 minutes until glossy."

Ham is already seasoned and cooked, so all it needs is a hot pan to crisp the edges. One shared base of onion, garlic and ham means you can choose rice or pasta depending on what is in the cupboard. Add the ham early enough that it browns rather than just warms.
