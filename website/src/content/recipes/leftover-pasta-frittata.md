  - "Heat the oil in a 20 cm (8 inch) ovenproof frying pan over medium-low heat. Pour in the mixture and cook, undisturbed, for 6 to 7 minutes until the edges are set and the base is golden."
  - "Finish under a hot grill for 2 to 3 minutes until the top is set and lightly browned. Or flip it with a plate and cook 2 minutes more."

Cooked pasta soaks up egg and turns the frittata into something sturdy enough to slice. Keep the heat gentle so the base browns without the middle turning rubbery. Any leftover vegetables or bits of cheese can go in too.
