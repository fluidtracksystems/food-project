  - "Mix the cold mash, egg, flour, cheese and spring onion in a bowl with a fork. Season with salt and pepper. The mixture should be soft but hold its shape."
  - "With floured hands, shape it into 6 patties about 2 cm (3/4 inch) thick."
  - "Heat the oil in a frying pan over medium heat. Fry the cakes for 4 to 5 minutes per side without moving them, until deep golden and crisp."
  - "Drain on paper towel and serve hot with a fried egg, a green salad or a spoonful of sour cream."

Cold mash is firm enough to shape, which is why this works better with leftovers than with fresh potato. Leave the cakes alone in the pan so a proper crust forms before you turn them.
