  - "Stir in the spinach and soy sauce and cook for 1 minute until the leaves wilt."
  - "While the oats cook, heat the oil in a small frying pan over medium heat and fry the egg until the white is set and the edges are crisp, about 3 minutes."
  - "Spoon the oats into a bowl, slide the egg on top and finish with the chili oil."

Think of it as a quick risotto made from oats. Cooking them in stock instead of milk turns them into a proper savory base, and the runny yolk makes its own sauce.
