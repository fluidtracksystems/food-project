steps:
  - "Beat the eggs with the milk and salt until no streaks of white remain."
  - "Melt the butter in a small nonstick pan over low to medium heat, until it foams but does not brown."
  - "Pour in the eggs and stir slowly with a spatula, pulling the curds across the pan, for about 3 minutes."
  - "Take the pan off the heat while the eggs look glossy and just set. They will finish cooking in the pan, and no liquid egg should remain."
  - "Spoon onto the toast, or into a bowl, and scatter over the herbs."

Low heat and constant gentle stirring give you small, creamy curds instead of dry, rubbery ones. Pull the pan off the heat a moment early, because the eggs keep cooking in their own warmth. It is a small plate that feels generous.
